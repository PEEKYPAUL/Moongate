# Moongate feature guide

Every feature in depth, with the version it arrived in. The [README](../README.md#features) has the short version.

Versions marked *vX.Y.Z+* need at least that app release; *plugin 0.6.N+* means the Pi plugin needs updating too (the tile shows a badge when it does).

- **Fleet dashboard** - live webcam thumbnails (the feed rate follows your network automatically - smoother on Wi-Fi, throttled on mobile data to save it - with separate rates you can tune for each, or switch a feed off entirely), print progress that matches what Mainsail shows **with the time left right on the tile** (or the projected finish time - switch the format, or hide it, in menu → Dashboard Layout), temperatures, a chamber sensor, and a per-printer status badge. Tiles auto-sort by activity so whatever's printing floats to the top - or turn that off and **drag them into your own order**, which sticks and travels in your backups. **Hide a printer's webcam** to shrink it to a compact tile, and the grid packs full and compact tiles together so the printers you're not watching take up less room - compact tiles keep the full quick-action row (status, pause/stop, files, control panel, lighting; v0.9.60+).
- **Single-printer dashboard** (v0.9.69+) - rather see one printer at a time? Tick **Single-printer dashboard** under menu → Dashboard Layout (a fresh install asks "One printer or several?" straight after the language) and the dashboard becomes one printer, full screen: a header that stays put while you scroll with the Local / Tunnel colour, the print state and the double-tap emergency stop, then the camera, the print job with pause and stop (or **Start a print** and **Preheat** when it's idle), the temperatures, big **Macros**, **Console** and **Print files** buttons with the control panel, file system and full Mainsail / Fluidd page beneath them, and the live **X Y Z position** with homed axes and a Home button. The arrows beside the menu step through your printers in your own order, a tap on the printer name lists them all, and with rotation allowed it splits into camera on the left, controls on the right. Untick it any time for the tile grid, now called the Multi-printer dashboard.
- **Multi-toolhead & tool changers** - IDEX and tool-changer printers (klipper-toolchanger / StealthChanger) are fully supported. The dashboard shows **every hotend** as its own **T0 / T1 / …** chip with the **active tool highlighted**, **preheat** sets a target for each tool at once (plus the bed), and the print notification lists **all** their live hotend temperatures. The Pi plugin folds the whole toolhead set into the single secure status call the app already reads, so detection stays reliable on Wi-Fi, over the tunnel, and on a VPN.
- **External cameras** - point a tile at a camera that isn't wired into Klipper (an old phone as a webcam, a network IP cam, a **go2rtc** feed). Cameras already configured in Mainsail are auto-detected - including **go2rtc / WebRTC** cameras (v0.9.52+, shown as live stills) - or set one by hand with the tile's gear. Works on Wi-Fi and remotely through the tunnel (home-network cameras), and a menu toggle hides the gears. **Several cameras on one printer?** A switch button on the tile and in full-screen picks which one to show (v0.9.59+ with plugin 0.6.22+); the choice sticks per printer, and a hand-set gear camera still wins.
- **Print controls** - pause, resume, and stop from the tile, plus a one-tap firmware restart for idle or errored printers. An **emergency-stop** triangle on every online tile halts the machine instantly (Klipper `M112`) on a **double-tap** - and once it's stopped, the triangle turns into a one-tap **restart** to bring the printer back. If a printer has a Moonraker **power device**, a power button on its tile switches it **on or off** (a popup offers Turn on and Turn off, and the button is greyed out while the printer is printing or paused) - and it works even while the printer is off, so you can wake it remotely. No power device? Point the printer at its own **power macro** instead (Advanced Power Switch) and the same button drives that. There's also an optional **Power all machines** button for the top bar (switch it on in the menu) that powers your whole fleet on or off at once, driving each printer however it's set up (a power device or a power macro), showing exactly which ones will switch, and always leaving a printer that's currently printing on.
- **Lighting control** - drive your printer's lights from the dashboard. Set each printer's on/off (or single toggle) macro under the **Lighting** menu, then tap the bulb on its camera (on a compact, webcam-hidden tile it sits in the action row instead). Point it at the light's Klipper object and the bulb shows the **real** on/off state, even when you switch the light elsewhere.
- **Print files on the printer** - tap the folder button on a ready printer to browse the G-code already saved on it, shown with slicer **thumbnails**, newest first. Pick one and **Start print** with a confirm tap - no slicer, no re-upload.
- **Control panel** (v0.9.64+) - the tile's macro button opens a panel you arrange per printer. Add, remove, reorder, rename and recolour **temperature**, **motion** and **macro** modules (or start from the Essential / Motion / Macros presets): set hotend and bed targets together with a one-tap heaters-off, home and jog the toolhead (0.1 / 1 / 10 mm, per-axis or all at once), extrude and retract, and turn **any Klipper macro into a labelled button** with its own icon, colour and parameters. The parameters a macro needs are suggested from the macro itself, and the exact command is shown for you to check before it runs. Your layout and buttons save per printer and travel in your backups. All of it is phone-to-Pi, on Wi-Fi and over the tunnel alike, with no plugin update.
- **Console & config editor** (v0.9.61+) - two buttons under every online printer's temperatures. **Console** opens the printer's live G-code console as an overlay: recent history already filled in, live updates while open, errors in red, quick-command chips and **command completions** (v0.9.64+) that fill the input but never send by themselves, and tap any past command to reuse it. It stays available mid-print and even while Klipper is in an error state - exactly when a console matters. **File System** browses the printer's configuration folder and opens `printer.cfg` (and friends) in a **structured editor**: setting names stay fixed as labels, you edit just the values, and your comments and layout survive a save untouched. The editor is **Klipper-aware** (v0.9.64+) - it suggests the right section and option names as you type, warns about values that don't look right before you save, and lets you edit a macro's own G-code inline. A backup copy is saved on the printer before your first change, **Save and restart** brings Klipper up on the new config and watches it come back - and if it doesn't, one tap restores the backup. The browser keeps itself tidy too: a **Hide backups & hidden files** checkbox (on by default, v0.9.63+) filters Klipper's SAVE_CONFIG snapshots, `.bak` files, other tools' timestamped config copies (crowsnest-style, `crowsnest.conf.2024-12-22-1121`; v0.9.64+ also opens them as plain text) and dotfiles out of the list until you untick it - the choice is remembered and travels in backups. Works on Wi-Fi and over the tunnel alike, even while Klipper itself is down, with no plugin update needed.
- **Full Mainsail / Fluidd UI** - tap a tile to open the complete web UI in-app; whichever you run is auto-detected. Every printer's page is **warmed in the background the moment the app starts and then kept loaded, so even the first time you open a printer it's instant** - no "Initializing…" reload, a big difference over the tunnel. A built-in full-screen **camera view** - open it from the top-bar icon, the tile's eye button, or just **press and hold any tile's camera** - runs at the camera's **full frame rate** with pinch-to-zoom, and keeps the feed working when you're away - even for an external camera the embedded page can't load over mobile data.
- **Auto local ↔ remote** - tries home WiFi first every poll, falls back to the Cloudflare tunnel within ~2s when you're away, and flips back to "Local" the moment you're home. Printers that are **switched off cost almost nothing in the background** - the dashboard and the print notifications both stop reaching out to them until they're back online - so it's easy on mobile data and battery. Prefer to stay off the internet entirely? An optional **Local only** switch in the top bar (enable its button in the menu) turns remote connections off with one tap - only printers on your own network connect until you tap it again.
- **Run it your way - Direct (LAN/VPN) mode** - a fully **cloud-free** way to run Moongate: install the Pi in LAN-only mode, add the printer by QR or address, and the app talks **straight to Moonraker** on your network - no account, no tunnel, nothing ever leaves your LAN. Away from home you connect over **your own VPN** (WireGuard, Tailscale). Existing printers **switch between cloud and Direct** from their edit dialog, no re-pairing. Honest trade-off: no print notifications in Direct mode, and remote access is your VPN's job. [Details ›](../README.md#run-it-your-way)
- **Print notifications** - opt-in live fleet status in your notification shade - per-printer progress, the projected **finish time**, temperatures and heat-up - with start / finish / pause / error alerts and a configurable refresh interval. The alerts cover the machine itself too: if Klipper errors out or shuts down - mid-print or idle - you get the alert **with the reason** ("Heater extruder not heating at expected rate"), even though that's exactly when a printer normally goes quiet. On iPhone they arrive as push notifications; on Android (v0.9.61+) a paused, failed or errored printer buzzes through its own **"Printer alerts"** notification category, which you can mute on its own without touching the quiet status bar. There's also a **`MOONGATE_NOTIFY MSG="your text"`** macro (plugin 0.6.25+): call it from any gcode - slicer end-gcode, a filament-runout handler - and the text lands on your phone (as a push on iPhone; on Android with plugin 0.6.26+, picked up by the notification service while it's running). **Choose which details show and drag them into the order you want**, and the roster follows your dashboard's order (your **Auto-arrange by status** setting). A **pause/play button** in the top bar suspends the background checks with one tap when your printers will be off for a while (saving battery), then resumes them when you tap it again. Off by default. The same service powers the **heat-soak alert** you arm from a tile's preheat sheet (long-press its temperatures, v0.9.67+): it fires the moment every temperature you set is really reached, or, with a soak time, once they have held that long - including a **chamber** temperature to wait for on printers that report a chamber sensor. **Since v0.9.68 (plugin 0.6.27+) the printer itself can do the watching**, so these reach iPhones too and the phone can be asleep: the *Start print?* dialog offers **Preheat and soak first** (heat the bed, and a chamber where there is a sensor, hold the soak time, then start the print by itself if the printer is still idle) and **Tell me when it's cool enough to remove** (an alert once the bed and chamber are back within 5° of where they were when you pressed Start), the tile shows "Soaking · 12 min left" meanwhile with a tap to cancel, and a **`MOONGATE_TEMP_NOTIFY BED= EXTRUDER= CHAMBER= SOAK= MSG=`** macro arms the same kind of watch from your own PRINT_START or PRINT_END without blocking the queue.
- **App lock** - optional PIN + biometric (fingerprint/face) on launch, with configurable auto-lock and screenshot protection. Off by default.
- **Guided tour** - an optional in-app walkthrough, offered once you've added your first printer, that points out each part of the app live: the connection bars and what they mean (including the "tunnel still building" marker you see just after pairing), the temperatures and the double-tap emergency stop, the console and file-system tools, the webcam and preheat, and the main options in the menu. Step forward or back, end it any time, and re-run it from the bottom of the menu. The tour always brings its own stage on screen (v0.9.62+): it jumps the dashboard to the top when it starts and shows your first printer as a healthy connected example, even if it's really offline.
- **9 languages** - fully translated into English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Polish, and Brazilian Portuguese (community-contributed); choose on first launch or anytime from the menu.
- **Themes & layout** - **Light / Dark / fully Custom** colours, plus **Phone colours** on **Android 12+** that follow your system **Material You** wallpaper palette and light/dark setting. Custom adds your own **dashboard background image** and adjustable **tile opacity**. Restyle the whole app's text with a **36-font picker** (grouped by style), and there's a 1-3 column grid, display-size scaling, optional landscape, and a switch to **hide the floating add / reorder buttons** at the bottom (menu → Dashboard Layout) when you have a lot of printers.
- **Backup & restore** - save your printers and settings to a file; restoring after a reinstall, or on a new phone, brings your printers **back online with no re-pairing**. Restoring **adds to** what's already on your dashboard - it never removes printers unless you choose to match the backup exactly.
- **In-app updates** (early-access APK; store builds update through their store) - when a new version lands, update right inside the app: it downloads with a progress bar and installs in place - no browser detour, nothing re-paired. The dashboard also keeps the **printer side** current: a printer running an out-of-date Moongate plugin shows a small **update icon on its tile** until it's updated - tap it and (on recent plugins) **one tap updates the plugin straight from your phone**; older plugins get pointed at Mainsail's Software Updates panel.

> 🔐 **Hardened remote access** - every internet-facing request is gated by a short-lived signed token. Leaking the tunnel URL alone gives an attacker nothing but flat `401`s, with no Mainsail/Moonraker fingerprint. [How it works ›](../README.md#how-it-works)
